Wrap Up

To pwn all the boxes we are provided, there are several important stuff we need to care about.

  • We need to do enumeration really carefully. If you miss an inmportant piece of information, you gonna stuck 2 hours at least. The step which requires the most concentration is enumeration.
  • The second most important stuff is to TAKE NOTE OF ALL INFORMATION, for instance, the possibility of CVE(If not seems to be feasible), password credentials, admin hashed etc.
  • Also, it's important to make routine, do not do random enumeration.
