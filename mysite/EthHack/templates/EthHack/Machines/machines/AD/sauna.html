└─$ echo '$IP egotistical-bank.local' | sudo -tee /a /etc/hosts

We have several stuff to do like enumerating smb shares, browsing webpage, checking ldap etc. Let's start with SMB enumeration.

Foothold

We created a wordlist to brute-force target machine's users, however, we have no password to do that.

In this context, we have few stuff can be done. Though we can do further website enumeration, I'll perform AS-REP roasting since there may be a user who have Do Not Require Kerberos Preauthentication setting.

for name in $(cat username.txt); do impacket-GetNPUsers egotistical-bank.local/$name -request -no-pass -dc-ip 10.129.95.180 >> hashes.asreproast ;done
