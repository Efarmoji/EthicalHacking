Why 7z?

The unzip command only supports older ZIP compression formats.
On the other hand, 7z supports newer encryption and compression algorithms, especially AES-256 encrypted ZIP files.
That's why 7z can extract the archive (because it supports that method), while unzip fails and shows the error “unsupported compression method 99.”
