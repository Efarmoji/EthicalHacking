What is Chisel?

Chisel is a lightweight TCP/UDP tunneller (single static binary) that tunnels arbitrary traffic over HTTP(S). Written in Go, it's commonly used for reverse tunnels, port forwarding, and pivoting through restrictive networks or proxies.

Background Chisel

./chisel client http://192.168.45.224:8001 R:12345:127.0.0.1:8000 &
  • Just attach &

Usage

On your machine

┌──(kali㉿kali)-[~/utils/Linux]
└─$ ./chisel server -p 8001 --reverse

Use 127.0.0.1

┌──(kali㉿kali)-[~/utils/Linux]
└─$ mysql --protocol=TCP -h 127.0.0.1 -P 12345 -u root -p
