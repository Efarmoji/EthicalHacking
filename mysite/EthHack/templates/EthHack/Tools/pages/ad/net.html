What is net?

This tool is part of the samba(7) suite. The Samba net utility is meant to work just like the net utility available for windows and DOS.

Subcommands for rpc

Commnad Role
group List/modify groups

Usage

Group

Show members

net rpc group members GROUP -U "DOMANIN_NAME"/"U_NAME"%"PASSWD" -S $IP

Add member

net rpc group addmem GROUP TARGET_USER -U "DOMAIN_NAME"/"USER"%"PASS" -S $IP
