Option Breakdown

  • swaks
    • SMTP testing tool: Swiss Army Knife for SMTP.
    • Used to send test emails, check authentication, attachments, headers, etc.
  • -t jim@relia.com
    • Sets the recipient (To) address.
    • Here, the email will be sent to jim@relia.com.
  • --from mark@relia.com
    • Sets the sender (From) address used in the email header.
    • The email will appear to come from mark@relia.com (spoofed sender).
  • --attach @config.Library-ms
    • Adds an attachment to the email.
    • @filename means “attach the contents of this file”.
    • Here, config.Library-ms is attached (likely for DLL/Library side-loading scenarios).
  • --server 192.168.212.189
    • Specifies the SMTP server to connect to.
    • In this case, swaks will connect directly to 192.168.212.189 on the SMTP port (usually 25/587).
  • --body @body.txt
    • Defines the email body (message content).
    • @body.txt means “use the contents of body.txt as the email body”.
    • Useful for pre-writing phishing text or instructions in a separate file.
  • --header "Subject: Antivirus soft installation"
    • Adds a custom header to the email.
    • Here it sets the Subject header to Antivirus soft installation.
    • This subject is crafted to look like an IT/security-related installation request.
  • --suppress-data
    • Tells swaks not to print the full message data (body, attachments) in the output.
    • Makes the terminal output cleaner by showing mostly the SMTP dialogue and status.
  • -ap
    • Enables SMTP authentication and prompts for credentials.
    • When you run the command, you see: 
      Username: maildmz@relia.com
Password: DPuBT9tGCBrTbR
    • These credentials are used to authenticate against the SMTP server before sending the email.
